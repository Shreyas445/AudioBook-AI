The tavern door creaked open, letting in a sudden rush of cold night air. 
The stranger stepped toward the fire, his boots thumping heavily against the floor.
 He looked at the innkeeper, let out a dry little laugh, and leaned in close.
  I have the gold, he whispered, pulling a heavy bag from his coat. 
  But then he caught his breath, his eyes darting to the window. 
  I was followed. Do you hear that? The forest... it’s finally waking up.
